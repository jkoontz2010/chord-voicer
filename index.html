Sing or hum one syllable per note, like da da da. A new take replaces the melody.

Pick a .mid file of a song. The app finds the melody, writes it out, and voices the chords the song plays so you can play along.

Song parts

Tap Listen to hear one part on its own, from where it comes in. The part that carries the tune is the one to use as the melody. Parts switched off are left out of Play song; the chords are still read from every part.
